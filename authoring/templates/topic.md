# Turning a topic into a question

What separates the two

> **Say:** Narration for this slide, written to be spoken rather than read.
> Sixty to ninety seconds spoken, so roughly 140 to 220 words, depending on how
> long the slide should stay on screen.

---

## Three tests for an answerable question

- It names what would count as an answer
- It can be wrong
- It fits the time you have

> **Say:** The bullets are signposts. The narration carries the content, so
> never read them aloud verbatim.
