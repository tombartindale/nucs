# Assignment 1 — Question and method

**What you are asked to do.** State the research question you propose to
investigate and the method you would use to answer it, and explain why that
method fits the question.

**What you submit.** One PDF of approximately 1,200 words, uploaded to the
course site.

**How it is judged.**

| Criterion | Adequate | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The question | Answerable and in scope | Names precisely what would count as an answer |
| The method | Appropriate to the question | Justified against a rejected alternative |
| The reasoning | Internally consistent | Acknowledges what the method cannot show |

**Outcomes addressed.** LO1, LO2.

**Before attempting this.** Units 1 to 3.
