# Unit 1 — Check your understanding

## Question 1

A researcher writes: "I want to study social media and teenagers." What is
missing?

- (a) A method → Not yet. The method follows the question, and there isn't a
  question here to follow.
- (b) An answerable question ✔ → Correct. This names a subject. Nothing in it
  says what would count as an answer.
- (c) A larger sample → No. Sample size is a later decision and cannot rescue
  an unstated question.
- (d) Ethical approval → No. Necessary eventually, but not what is missing.
