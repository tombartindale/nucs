# Assignment 1 — A research proposal

**What you are asked to do.** Write a short proposal that turns a topic of your choice into a
research question, and explain the design you would use to answer it.

**What you submit.** One PDF of approximately 1,000 words.

**How it is judged.**

| Criterion | Adequate | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The question | Answerable and in scope | Names precisely what would count as an answer |
| The design | Appropriate to the question | Justified against a rejected alternative |

**Outcomes addressed.** LO1, LO2.

**Before attempting this.** Units 1 to 3.
